# Organic launch content — drafts

Use after the matching features have been deployed and tested on the actual Play Store app. All figures below are fictional examples. No posts or messages have been sent. Do not describe them as real earnings or customer results.

## A clear offer

“KeeperBMA helps creators and side hustlers organize income, expenses, and monthly net income.”

Free entry points after deployment:
- https://keeperbma.com/profit-calculator.html — quick aggregate calculation, no signup.
- https://keeperbma.com/demo.html — interactive fictional income tracking demo.

Keep existing prices accurate when posting: Basic is currently $2/month and Regular is $7/month in this codebase. Regular adds supported bank connections; it is not required to use the manual creator report. Do not advertise an unimplemented $7 founding-customer offer.

## Six short videos

1. **Revenue isn't what you keep.** Show fictional $370 income, $25 software, and $45 equipment. Show the $300 net income report. End: “Try the free calculator in my profile.”
2. **Where did my side hustle money go?** Enter a $50 fictional sponsorship and compare it with monthly expenses. End: “I built a tracker for this. The demo needs no signup.”
3. **The subscriptions behind a creator channel.** Explain that editing, hosting, and music subscriptions belong in an expense review. Use fictional amounts, without suggesting they are automatically detected.
4. **Three sources, one monthly view.** Show YouTube, sponsorships, and freelance categories. State clearly that these are manual categories, not automatic connections to those platforms.
5. **Do you know your monthly net income?** Open the free calculator, change expenses, and show how the income needed for a $200 goal changes. Call the result a calculation, not a guaranteed earning plan.
6. **I need five people to test this.** Show the actual demo. Ask: “Can you record one sample transaction and tell me what was confusing?” Mention that feedback is welcome, without asking for a positive review.

Format: one problem, one screen recording, one useful takeaway, one invitation. Start on the channel where you already have an audience. Reuse a successful idea with a different example; do not create more channels just to repeat the same pitch.

## Tester invitation template

Use only for people who welcome the conversation or in communities where promotion is permitted:

“I'm building a simple income and expense tracker for side hustlers. Would you be willing to try a two-minute fictional-data demo and tell me where you get stuck? There is no signup for the demo. I built it, and I'm looking for feedback.”

Do not send bulk unsolicited messages. Ask about their workflow before asking for a subscription.

## Five interview questions

1. How do you currently track money from your side hustle?
2. What did you last have trouble finding or calculating?
3. Can you show how you would record a sample payout and expense here?
4. Which part would make you return next month?
5. Would you pay for this specific workflow? What would need to improve first?
