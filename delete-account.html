Delete Your KeeperBMA Account

How to remove your KeeperBMA account and what happens next.

Open Website Settings Email Support

How to Request Deletion

  1. Sign in to the KeeperBMA website.
  2. Open your settings or account management page.
  3. Use the account deletion option if it is available for your account.
  4. If you cannot access your account, email support@keeperbma.com and include the email address or username linked to the account.

What Deletion Means

  • Your account access is removed.
  • Your profile and related budgeting records are scheduled for deletion from active systems.
  • Some limited records may be retained for legal obligations, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or backup recovery windows.

Need Help First?

If you only need billing help, password recovery, or profile changes, contact support before deleting your account.
