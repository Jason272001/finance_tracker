KeeperBMA Account Help

Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your KeeperBMA account and associated app data by contacting our support team.

Effective date: May 12, 2026
Support email: support@keeperbma.com

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email support@keeperbma.com from the email address on your account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My KeeperBMA Account.
  3. Include your account email and any details that help us verify ownership.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your KeeperBMA account profile.
  • Your app data, including personal finance records and related saved information associated with your account, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.
  • Business workspace data associated only with your account, unless shared business ownership or legal retention requires separate review.

What May Be Retained

  • Records we must keep for legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, or dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Limited backup data retained for a short recovery window before secure cleanup.

Processing Time

We aim to review deletion requests promptly. Most verified requests are processed within 7 to 14 business days.

Need Help First?

If you are having trouble signing in or want help with subscriptions, billing, or account recovery before deleting your account, contact us at support@keeperbma.com.
