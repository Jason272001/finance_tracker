KeeperBMA Privacy Policy

How KeeperBMA handles account, financial, and support data across the website and mobile app.

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as your name, email address, phone number, username, and password hash.
  • Financial records you create or sync, including accounts, balances, transactions, categories, notes, and daily balance snapshots.
  • Subscription and access details such as plan, billing cycle, payment status, trial status, and coupon usage when applicable.
  • Bank connection information when you choose to connect institutions through supported banking providers.
  • Support and recovery information, including password recovery requests and messages you send to support.

How We Use Information

  • To provide budgeting, transaction tracking, account management, and plan-based features.
  • To sync your information across the website and mobile app.
  • To secure your account, investigate abuse, and support password recovery.
  • To process subscriptions, apply coupons, and manage account access.
  • To respond to support requests and improve service reliability.

Service Providers

KeeperBMA may use trusted service providers to operate the service, such as hosting and database providers, payment processors, and bank connectivity partners. When those providers are used, they receive only the information needed to perform their service.

Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and maintain secure backups. Retention periods may differ by data type and legal requirement.

Your Choices

  • You can review and manage account settings from the KeeperBMA website.
  • You can request account deletion by following the instructions on the account deletion page.
  • You can contact support if you need help accessing, correcting, or deleting your information.
Need help?

Email support@keeperbma.com or visit Account Deletion Help.

Contact

If you have privacy questions about KeeperBMA, contact support@keeperbma.com.
