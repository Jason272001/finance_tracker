Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how KeeperBMA collects, uses, and protects information when you use the KeeperBMA mobile app, website, and related services.

Effective date: May 12, 2026
Company: KeeperBMA
Website: keeperbma.com

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and login information.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, including phone number or business details.
  • Financial app content you create, such as accounts, transactions, notes, categories, and reports.
  • Subscription and billing status needed to manage access to paid features.
  • Support messages you send to KeeperBMA, including messages to our AI support assistant.
  • Basic technical data such as device type, app version, browser type, and error logs used to keep the app working properly.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide budgeting, business, and account management features.
  • To sync your data across supported devices and sessions.
  • To support subscriptions, coupons, and website-managed billing access.
  • To improve app stability, security, and user support.
  • To respond to customer service or account recovery requests.

Payments and Purchases

Subscriptions and payments are managed securely on our website. The mobile app does not provide in-app purchases. Payment providers may process billing information directly under their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

KeeperBMA does not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, error monitoring, or payment services, only when needed to provide the service.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect account access, session security, and stored data.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your Choices

  • You can update your profile information from within the app or website.
  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can contact us to request account deletion or data access help.

Children’s Privacy

KeeperBMA is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
