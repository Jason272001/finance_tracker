THE BUSINESS WORKSPACE

One place for the work around your business.

These connected workflows are available to explore with fictional data in the public demo. They are not yet live business operations.

Interactive demo
01 / SELL

POS & inventory

Build a catalog with product photos, descriptions, prices, brands, categories and CSV imports. Receive supplier purchases and watch stock update.

  • Demo cashier sign-in and operator names on sales and purchases.
  • Checkout, discounts, promotions, receipts, voids and loyalty redemption.
Explore the POS →
02 / ORGANIZE

Staff & store branches

Keep each branch’s team, shifts and time records together. Assign one shift to several staff and review planned hours.

  • Clock-in, unpaid breaks, clock-out, timesheet review and CSV export.
  • Gross-pay estimates with editable assumptions; no payroll payments or withholding.
Explore the team tools →
03 / RETAIN

Customers & loyalty

Keep contacts, referral sources, notes and follow-up tasks together. Preview optional phone membership and customer purchase history.

  • Owner-adjustable earning and redemption rates; 100 points = $1 by default.
  • Opt-in promotion conversations and STOP replies are simulations. No SMS is sent.
Explore customer tools →
04 / UNDERSTAND

Accounting & budgets

Connect sample sales, discounts, product costs and expenses in a profit-and-loss overview and activity ledger.

  • Budgets, inventory cost, tax collected, review checklists and CSV exports.
  • An accounting overview and tax organizer—not complete books or tax filing.
Explore the finance tools →
05 / GROW

Marketing & daily work

Plan campaigns and record spend, leads, orders and attributed revenue. Keep meeting agendas, assigned tasks and customer follow-ups visible.

  • Editable sharing invitations and labeled demo links for your channels.
  • No automatic messages, social posting or advertising purchases.
Explore the marketing tools →
06 / GET ORIENTED

Workspace controls & help

Try business profile and employee-access screens, switch between light and dark themes, and ask Omar about features, prices and demo steps.

  • The public guide explains what works today and which tools are previews.
  • Real AI answers need operator setup; demo PINs do not secure real staff accounts.
Explore the workspace →

FUTURE PROJECTS

Where KeeperBMA is heading.

A direction for development, not a delivery promise. These capabilities are not unlocked by a demo or guaranteed by subscribing.

Planned / future direction

Live business operations

Persistent POS, inventory and branch data, secure individual staff accounts, server-side permissions and auditable transactions.

Connected customer experiences

Verified phone membership, secure customer access and consent-based messaging through a production provider. Delivery, security and provider setup still need implementation.

Deeper financial workflows

Complete bookkeeping and more comprehensive payroll and tax integrations need additional design, jurisdiction-specific support and professional review.

Business websites & smarter tools

Public business pages, website publishing, expanded management tools, AI business analysis and image optimization remain planned features.
